Ever since I have disabled all the endless scrolling feed from my laptop.
_I have been visiting [lobste.rs](https://lobste.rs), [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com), [bubbles.town](https://bubbles.town) and my RSSReader more often._
I do have `Tuba` installed but the scroll is close to none. While there still is some discourse hapenning. 
I can feel that it is much calmer. I know that there will be nothing new if I reload. And I can consume(skim) almost all of it in one sitting.

Then, my laptop begins to look like a tool and not a media machine. It's the silence after that, which allows me to hear the birds chirping outside.
