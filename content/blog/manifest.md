<div style="text-align: left">

_lost,  
what you never owned.  
still claiming for it_

_blamed,  
what you couldn't control.  
still believing it did_

_saw,  
what you wanted to see.  
kept looking for it_

_found,  
what you feared the most.  
kept searching for it_

</div>
