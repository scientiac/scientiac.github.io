_What do you feel?  
When your heart suddenly stops.  
And your breaths aren't enough.  
The time has now, slowed down.  
Tried ignoring you, I can't somehow._  

_What would you say?  
When she's only just a text far.  
And you're afraid of her scars.  
The poet's eyes, is where things hide.
A glass hearted romance, as she writes._

_The worst that can happen?  
When she reads, a creepy romance.  
Hope she understands, for this once.
Of my dreams tonight, the worst one,
She'd respond but, the night's long gone._
